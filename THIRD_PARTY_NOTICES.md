# Third-party components

Manny Browser is a new application using Android WebView and AndroidX. It does not contain the Fulguris application's source. The existing Fulguris baseline and its pull request are preserved separately.

The Gradle wrapper is from Gradle v8.7.0, under Apache License 2.0. Its source headers are retained. AndroidX dependencies are Apache License 2.0, with their notices distributed in their artifacts. Android WebView is provided by the device and includes Chromium third-party notices through its provider.
